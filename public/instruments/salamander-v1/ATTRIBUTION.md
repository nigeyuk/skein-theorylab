# Sample credits

**Salamander Grand Piano v3** was recorded by **Alexander Holm** on a Yamaha C5 grand piano and is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The [full licence text](LICENSE.txt) accompanies the recordings.

- Original release: [Salamander Grand Piano v3](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3).
- Source for the exact recordings used here: the [SFZ Instruments FLAC distribution](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/Samples), with FLAC packaging and SFZ remapping by kinwie. Its [licence](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/blob/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/LICENSE) and [author credits](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/blob/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/README.md) were verified at that revision.
- MyTheoryLab adaptation: selected 16 roots at source velocity layers 4 and 13, trimmed each recording to 4.5 seconds with a half-second fade, downmixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz and encoded at 64 kb/s MP3. The [manifest](manifest.json) maps each adapted file to its source filename. The adapted recordings retain CC BY 3.0.
